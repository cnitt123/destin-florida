Welcome to Destin

Destin grew from a fishing community into a favorite Gulf Coast getaway. The harbor remains central to local life, while the beaches and clear water draw visitors year-round. Discover more through VISIT FLORIDA and the official visitor guide.

Beach tip: Check beach flags, surf conditions, parking rules, and park hours before heading out. Stay on marked boardwalks to protect dunes and sea oats.

Fishing roots, coastal character

🎣 The harbor

Destin's harbor supports commercial fishing, charter trips, waterfront dining, and local businesses.

🌊 East Pass

The pass connects Choctawhatchee Bay with the Gulf and shapes the area's boating and fishing culture.

🐬 On the water

Dolphin cruises, snorkeling, fishing trips, and boat rentals are popular ways to explore the coast.

Learn more at the City of Destin, Destin-Fort Walton Beach, and VISIT FLORIDA.
